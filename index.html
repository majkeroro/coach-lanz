FAQ

I'm a complete beginner. Can I join?

Yes — most start with zero experience. First 2 weeks are basics only: movement, breathing, light technique. No sparring, no pressure.

What do I need for the first session / online coaching?

Workout clothes, towel, water. For online: phone stand + 2x2m space + optional dumbbells or bands. Hand wraps / gloves guidance provided if you pick striking fitness.

Do I have to spar if I pick Muay Thai / Boxing fitness?

No. Striking fitness is pad + bag + footwork for cardio and confidence. Sparring is 100% optional, light, supervised and gear-required.

How fast will I see results?

With 2–3x/week + meal + sleep habits, most see 3–5kg fat loss / month or visible strength gains in 6–8 weeks. Recovery (sleep, stiffness, energy) often improves in 2–3 weeks.

Do you coach seniors or people with old injuries?

Yes — that’s a specialty. Joint-friendly strength, balance and mobility. We start easy, progress safely, and coordinate with your doctor/physio if needed. Not medical advice — coaching only.

How do online sessions work?

Video call + custom program + form video review + weekly Messenger check-ins. Same tracking as in-person.
