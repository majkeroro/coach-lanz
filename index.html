FAQ

I'm a complete beginner. Can I train with you?

Absolutely — most clients start from zero. Your first sessions cover basics only: movement, form, and light technique. No pressure, no sparring unless you want it.

How does online coaching work?

We start with your free consultation, then you get a personalized training + nutrition plan. You send form videos, I review them, and we check in weekly to adjust. Same accountability as in person.

What does the free consultation include?

15 minutes: goal assessment, honest evaluation of where you are, and a starter plan (training + nutrition basics). No payment, no obligation to continue.

How fast will I see results?

With 3x/week training plus the nutrition plan, fat-loss clients typically see 3–5kg/month; strength clients add visible muscle in 8–12 weeks. Consistency + check-ins make it happen.

What is the Muay Thai instructor certification?

Official instructor courses run by the Philippine Thai Boxing Association in Cavite. Message me to ask about the next batch, requirements, and slots.
