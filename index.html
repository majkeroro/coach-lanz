Questions

Before you ask.

I'm a complete beginner. Can I train with you?

Absolutely — most clients start from zero. First sessions cover basics only: movement, form, light technique. No pressure, no sparring unless you want it.

How does online coaching work?

Free consultation first, then a personalized training + nutrition plan. You send form videos, I review them, and we check in weekly. Same accountability as in person.

What does the free consultation include?

15 minutes: goal assessment, honest evaluation, and a starter plan with training + nutrition basics. No payment, no obligation.

How fast will I see results?

With 3x/week training plus the nutrition plan, fat-loss clients typically see 3–5kg/month; strength clients add visible muscle in 8–12 weeks.

What is the instructor certification?

Official Muay Thai instructor courses run by the Philippine Thai Boxing Association in Cavite. Message me about the next batch and slots.
