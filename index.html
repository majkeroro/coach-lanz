FAQ

I'm a complete beginner. Can I join?

Yes — 70% start with zero experience. First 2 weeks are basics only: stance, footwork, light pads. No sparring until you're ready and coach-approved.

What do I bring for trial?

Workout shirt, shorts, towel, water. We lend hand wraps for trial. Boxing/Muay Thai gloves available for rent/purchase.

Will sparring hurt me?

Light, technical sparring only, with headgear/shinguards and coach supervision. You can opt out — most members never spar and still get fit.

How fast will I lose weight?

With 3x/week training + our meal guide, members average 3–5kg/month sustainably. We track weigh-ins monthly for free.

Do you offer ladies-only or kids classes?

Yes — beginners and ladies train safely in all classes, plus Kids Muay Thai every Sat–Sun 8AM. Message us for ladies-only PT slots.
